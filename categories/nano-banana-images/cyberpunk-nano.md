# Nano Banana: Cyberpunk Micro-Tech 🍌⚡

## Objective
Generate a high-detail, macroscopic view of a "Nano Banana" that looks like a piece of advanced cyberpunk technology.

## The Prompt
> **Macro-photography of a miniature, ultra-high-tech banana, 2 inches long. The skin is made of brushed titanium with glowing neon blue circuitry patterns etched into its surface. Instead of a stem, there is a small USB-C port and a microscopic cooling fan. The banana sits on a circuit board background with shallow depth of field. Soft purple and blue ambient lighting, cinematic, 8k resolution, photorealistic, intricate textures, metallic finish.**

## Recommended Model
- Midjourney v6
- DALL-E 3
- Stable Diffusion XL
